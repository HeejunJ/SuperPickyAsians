Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

This policy explains what information SuperPickyAsians ("we", "us") collects when you use our iOS app and website (the "Service"), how we use it, and the choices you have. If you have questions, email pixiefitscs@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

Account information. When you create an account we collect your email address, the display name you choose, and — if you sign in with Google or Apple — the basic profile information those services share with us (name and email; Apple may give us a private relay email instead).

Content you create. Reviews (star ratings, text, tags, whether you were a local or visiting), photos you attach, comments, likes, and Community stories. Reviews, photos, comments and stories are public and shown with your display name.

Picky test and preferences. Your answers to the Picky test, your resulting Picky Score, and the priorities you set (taste, portion, value, atmosphere, service). Your Picky Score is shown next to your reviews.

Blocked accounts. If you block someone, we keep the list of accounts you blocked (and their display names) with your account so we can hide their content from you. Only you can see it.

Imported reviews. If you choose to import your own Google Maps reviews, we read the file you select on your device and post only the reviews you confirm. We do not keep the file.

Technical information. A random user ID created when you first open the app (even before you sign in), and basic information our hosting providers log to operate and secure the Service (such as IP address and device/browser type).

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or advertising identifiers, and we do not sell your personal information.

2. How we use information

  • To run the Service: sign you in, show your reviews and stories, calculate your Picky Score, and personalize match scores and recommendations.
  • To keep the community safe: review reports, remove content that breaks our Terms & Community Guidelines, and prevent abuse.
  • To answer your support requests and send essential service messages (for example, a password-reset email you requested).

3. Service providers

We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage and Hosting) to store and serve data, with servers located in the United States. If you sign in with Google or Apple, those companies process your sign-in under their own privacy policies. Place pages may show a live rating from Google Maps; that request is made by your device to Google, and we do not store Google's ratings.

4. Retention and deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. You can delete any review, photo or comment you posted at any time. You can delete your whole account in the app under Me → Delete my account; this removes your account, your reviews and their photos, and clears your Picky test results. Comments and Community stories can be deleted one by one before you delete your account, or we will remove them if you ask. Copies may remain in backups for a limited time before they are overwritten. You can also email us to request deletion.

5. Your choices and rights

You can access and update your information in the app, and ask us for a copy of your data or its correction or deletion by emailing pixiefitscs@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law; we will respond to requests as those laws require.

6. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and protected by access rules that limit who can read or change it. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. If the changes are significant, we will let you know in the app before they take effect. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
